What these workshops share

the common thread across agents, apps, and data science

They are for builders.

These are practical, technical workshops for people who need to make AI systems work in real workflows. The emphasis is architecture, iteration, evaluation, and judgment.

  • Engineers building AI applications and agent systems
  • Data scientists and analysts adopting agentic workflows
  • Founders and technical leaders who need first-principles clarity

They are not prompt-tip sessions.

The goal is not to memorize a bag of tricks. It is to understand how the pieces fit together so you can adapt when models, tools, and products change.

  • Tool use, memory, control flow, and context engineering
  • Evals, debugging, observability, and production habits
  • Human-in-the-loop workflows and operational safety

Want this for your team?

The public workshops are for individual learners and open cohorts. For company-specific sessions, the preserved team-workshops page has the B2B/private workshop material.
