Beyond prototypes.

the operational layer
These workshops are for teams moving from experimentation into operational reality: software systems, internal tooling, product workflows, and engineering practice.

We focus on evals, context, harnesses, observability, debugging, verification, and human-in-the-loop workflows. The operational layer that determines whether AI systems become useful or collapse under ambiguity.

Core workshops.

three flagship intensives
01 · production
Building Reliable Systems with AI
  • From prototype to production
  • Evals, observability, and verification
  • Context engineering and debugging
  • Human-in-the-loop systems and production habits
The broad operational lens. For teams shipping AI-powered software whose demos haven't survived contact with users.
02 · engineering
Agentic Engineering Workflows
  • How software engineering changes when agents participate
  • Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, MCP, background reviewer agents
  • AI-assisted coding and debugging workflows
  • Human-agent collaboration and workflow redesign
For engineering teams rebuilding how they ship. Tools are examples. The work is workflow design.
03 · agents
Building AI Agents for the Enterprise
  • Tool use, memory, orchestration, harnesses
  • Delegation and long-running systems
  • Approval loops and operational safety
  • Enterprise constraints and production guardrails
For teams putting agents into production. Where reliability, safety, and operational discipline meet.
